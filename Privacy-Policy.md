# AutoCallSMS Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 20, 2026

AutoCallSMS is an Android application that provides automatic SMS replies based on phone call activity.

## Permissions

AutoCallSMS may request:

- Phone State – to detect call status.
- Call Log – to identify relevant call activity.
- SMS – to send the automatic reply configured by the user.
- Receive Boot Completed – to allow the app to continue its configured functionality after the device restarts.

## Data Collection

AutoCallSMS does not intentionally collect or sell users' personal information.

Information accessed through phone and SMS permissions is used only to provide the application's features.

## SMS

When automatic reply is enabled, the application may send an SMS using the message configured by the user.

The user can disable the automatic reply feature at any time.

## Data Sharing

AutoCallSMS does not intentionally sell or share call logs, phone information, or SMS content with third parties.

## User Control

Users can disable the automatic reply feature or revoke application permissions through Android settings.

## Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by the application. However, no electronic system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's features or data practices change.
